# Demo Store Customer Policies

Source: policies.md. These are fictional learning policies, not the UCI dataset merchant's terms. They must not be applied as actual entitlements to the 2010–2011 historical invoices.

## Shipping
Processing normally takes 1–2 business days. Standard shipping normally takes 3–5 business days after shipment. These are estimates, not guaranteed delivery dates. The UCI data has no shipping status or delivery dates, so no actual delivery estimate is available.

## Returns
A return may be requested within 30 days of delivery for unused items in their original packaging. Final eligibility requires purchase and delivery details and staff review. The UCI database has no delivery dates, so the assistant cannot confirm eligibility or compute a return deadline. Collect the customer's explanation and offer a support ticket.

## Refunds
Approved refunds normally take 5–10 business days after the returned item is inspected. This application cannot approve or execute refunds. A local ticket is a request for review, not a refund approval.

## Cancellation
Orders still processing may be eligible for cancellation after staff review. Shipped orders cannot be cancelled under this demo policy. This application cannot change or cancel an order; it can record a support request.

## Escalation
With the customer's confirmation, record a local ticket linked to their order. Tickets start with status "open". No email is sent and no real support team is notified. Never ask for passwords or complete payment-card details.
